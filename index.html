EXPERIENCE HAPUTALE

Beyond your room.
Into the hills.

Tea-country viewpoints, peaceful heritage and waterfall outings. These places around Haputale are ideas for your own itinerary, not included hotel tours.

01

Tea country & viewpoints

Lipton’s Seat

Look out across rolling tea estates from the viewpoint associated with Sir Thomas Lipton. The journey through the Dambatenne tea country is part of the appeal, with green slopes and hill-country panoramas along the way.

Plan for clear weather; mist can hide the view. Confirm local road access before setting out.

View map ↗Visitor information ↗
02

Heritage & quiet gardens

Adisham Bungalow

Discover a historic country house that later became a Benedictine monastery. Its stone architecture, gardens and peaceful setting offer a different side of Haputale, close to the Thangamale area.

This is a religious property. Check visitor opening arrangements in advance and respect restricted areas.

View map ↗Visitor information ↗
03

Hiking & tea landscapes

The Pekoe Trail

Stage 13 connects Haputale with St. Catherine through the hill country. The official route lists tea estates and viewpoints along the way, making it an option for guests who want to explore the landscape on foot.

Check the official trail information, access requirements and weather; choose a route suited to your fitness.

View map ↗Visitor information ↗
04

Mountain scenery & village life

Idalgashinna

Discover the mountain setting around Idalgashinna and its railway station. The area features on Stage 12 of the Pekoe Trail, alongside the ridge landscapes on the approach towards Haputale.

Use designated walking routes rather than railway tracks. Check the official route before planning a hike.

View map ↗Visitor information ↗
05

Waterfall outing

Diyaluma Falls

Plan an outing towards Koslanda to see Diyaluma’s dramatic cascade and surrounding green landscape. A waterfall-viewing trip can be a scenic change from Haputale’s tea-covered hills.

Check conditions locally. Rocks can be slippery; stay away from cliff edges and do not assume pools are safe to swim in.

View map ↗Visitor information ↗
06

Waterfalls & forest scenery

Bambarakanda Falls

Bambarakanda is Sri Lanka’s tallest waterfall, set among the hill-country landscape near Kalupahana. It is an option for a separate waterfall excursion, with forest scenery adding to the appeal.

Arrange transport and check current path access and weather before travelling.
